# 8mb.local Android Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

8mb.local's Android app processes media locally on the user's device. It does
not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, accounts, or telemetry, and
it does not request Android's Internet permission.

## Media access and processing

The app uses Android's system Photo Picker. It can access only the videos the
user selects. Selected media is read locally for compression or audio
extraction. Finished videos are saved to the device camera roll under
`DCIM/8mb.local`; finished audio is saved under `Music/8mb.local` unless the
user chooses another location.

## Data storage and sharing

Compression history and settings remain in the app's local storage. 8mb.local
does not transmit selected media, history, settings, or device identifiers to
JMS1717 or to an 8mb.local server. Sharing occurs only when the user explicitly
opens Android's Share interface and chooses another app or service; that
recipient's privacy practices then apply.

## Deletion

Users can clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove its private
history and settings. Exported media remains in the user's chosen media folder
until the user deletes it through Android or a file/gallery app.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be recorded in this repository. Questions can be opened
at <https://github.com/JMS1717/8mb.local/issues>.

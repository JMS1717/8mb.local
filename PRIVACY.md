# Privacy Policy for 8mb.local

**Last updated:** August 10, 2026  
**Publisher:** jms1717  
**Product:** 8mb.local (Microsoft Store / desktop)

## Summary

8mb.local is a **local video compression app**. On Windows, it processes media on your computer and does **not** upload your videos to our servers, does **not** require an account, and does **not** include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

## What the app does with your files

- You choose video (or related media) files to compress.
- The app reads those files and writes compressed outputs under your Windows user profile, typically in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\8mb.local` (settings, history metadata, uploads, and outputs).
- Processing uses a local service bound to `127.0.0.1` (localhost only).
- Compressed files and history stay on your device unless you choose to move, share, or delete them yourself.

## Personal information

The Microsoft Store edition of 8mb.local is designed **not to collect, transmit, or sell personal information** to the publisher.

It may store **on your device only**:

- App settings you configure
- Optional local job history (for example file names, sizes, and status)
- Temporary upload/output media you process
- Local diagnostic logs useful for troubleshooting on your machine

That data remains local. Uninstalling the Store package does not necessarily delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\8mb.local`, so history and outputs can persist across reinstalls or alternate installers. You can delete that folder manually to remove local app data.

## Network use

The desktop app may use the network only for ordinary Windows/platform behavior (for example Microsoft Store updates) or optional features you explicitly use. Normal compression of local files does not send your media to jms1717.

If you run other deployment modes of this open-source project (for example self-hosted Docker on a server), privacy depends on how **you** host and expose that instance. This policy covers the **publisher-distributed Windows Store / desktop product**.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Do not use it to process sensitive personal media if you are not comfortable storing that media on the local device.
